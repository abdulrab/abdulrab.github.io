Question 3 - Fixed-point iteration                                                            [10 marks]
The equation
                                              x3 − x − 2 = 0
can be written in fixed-point form as

                                          x = g(x) = (x + 2)1/3 .

Use the interval [1, 2] and the initial approximation p0 = 1.5.

 (a) Use Theorem 2.3 to show that g has a unique fixed point in [1, 2]. In particular, verify that
     g([1, 2]) ⊆ [1, 2] and find a constant k < 1 such that |g ′ (x)| ≤ k on (1, 2).   [4 marks]
 (b) Compute p1 , p2 , p3 , p4 using pn = g(pn−1 ).                                            [3 marks]
 (c) Using Corollary 2.5 and the bound

                                       |pn − p| ≤ k n max{p0 − a, b − p0 },

      estimate the smallest n that guarantees |pn − p| < 10−4 .                                [3 marks]
