Research interests

Numerical analysis, numerical methods for partial differential equations, hyperbolic conservation laws, computational mathematics, mathematical modeling, and scientific computing.

Numerical Solution to the Riemann Problem for the Liquid–Gas Two-Phase Isentropic Flow Model

2022

M.S. thesis · Sukkur IBA University · Supervised by Dr. Sidrah Ahmed

  • Studied a one-dimensional two-phase flow model governed by nonlinear hyperbolic conservation laws with Riemann initial data.
  • Implemented and compared the Lax–Friedrichs, Lax–Wendroff, and FORCE schemes in Python.
  • Examined how each scheme resolved smooth profiles, shocks, and rarefaction waves, with attention to numerical dissipation and spurious oscillations.

Mathematical Modeling and Simulation of HIV Dynamics

2019

B.S. thesis · Sukkur IBA University · Supervised by Dr. Syed Hyder Ali Muttaqi Shah

  • Developed mathematical models of within-host HIV dynamics that incorporated antiretroviral treatment.
  • Studied equilibria and local stability using linearization and the Hartman–Grobman theorem, and ran simulations with the classical fourth-order Runge–Kutta method.
