Research

Numerical mathematics with a computational focus.

My research experience centers on mathematical models governed by differential equations, their qualitative structure, and numerical approximation.

Research direction

I am particularly interested in numerical analysis and scientific computing for differential-equation models. My current direction is toward numerical methods for hyperbolic systems, conservation laws, Riemann problems, and multiphase-flow models.

My goal is to deepen the analysis of numerical schemes while developing reliable computational implementations that can resolve shocks, rarefactions, and other nonlinear flow features.

Selected research

2022 · M.S. RESEARCH / PREPRINT

Numerical Solution to the Riemann Problem for the Liquid–Gas Two-Phase Isentropic Flow Model

A nonlinear coupled PDE system involving liquid density, gas density, and liquid velocity was solved numerically on a 1D space–time grid using Python.

Methods: Lax–Friedrichs, Lax–Wendroff, and FORCE schemes; comparison of smooth profiles, shock waves, rarefaction, and numerical oscillations.

arXiv:2512.02071 ↗
2019 · B.S. RESEARCH / PREPRINT

Mathematical Modeling and Simulation of HIV Dynamics

Within-host HIV models describing healthy CD4+ T cells, infected cells, and free virus, including treatment-control parameters for reverse transcriptase and protease inhibitors.

Methods: equilibria, local stability through linearization and the Hartman–Grobman theorem, and fourth-order Runge–Kutta simulations.

arXiv:2512.13171 ↗
